Welcome to FertilityScope

This interactive map tracks and predicts urban soil fertility across Bangalore using AI.

Map Markers (Sites)
Each glowing point represents a specific geographic site. Clusters with numbers show multiple sites grouped together; click them to zoom in.
Fertility Forecasts
Click any marker to open its analytics panel. You'll see historical fertility trends and a 6-month future forecast powered by Deep Learning models.
Interactive Filters
Use the legend in the bottom right to filter sites by their current fertility status (Optimal, Moderate, or Low).
